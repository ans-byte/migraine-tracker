What's Included in Reports
Migraine Summary
Total count, average severity, average duration, and detailed log of each migraine episode.
Sleep Statistics
Average hours slept, sleep quality score, and nightly sleep log entries.
Water Intake
Total cups consumed, average daily intake, and daily breakdown.
Stress Levels
Average stress level, high/low points, and daily stress log entries.
How to Use Reports
  1. CSV files can be opened in Excel, Google Sheets, or any spreadsheet application for further analysis.
  2. PDF files are formatted for printing or sharing with your healthcare provider.
  3. For weekly reports, select the Monday of the week you want to report on.
  4. For monthly reports, select the month and year from the dropdowns.
  5. Reports include only data you have logged in the tracker — log consistently for the most useful reports.
